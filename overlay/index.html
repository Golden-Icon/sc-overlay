SC Overlay

pick your widgets, size them, move them, stack them
Drag a panel's header (or its ⣿ grip) to move it; drag the bottom-right corner to size it, the right edge for width only. Panels overlap freely and whichever one you moved last is on top, so drag a covering panel aside to reach what is under it. A panel's ▸ button lists the widgets you can stack behind it — tick one to add it, untick to take it back out. Everything here is remembered by this browser only, and none of it changes what the overlay shows in-game.
